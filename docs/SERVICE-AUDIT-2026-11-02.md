- **Observed:** Related service links were chosen by broad category and the `Operate` category. That missed obvious cloud/recovery/documentation relationships. An explicit three-service map now gives every service a deliberate path to adjacent work.

- **Observed:** Case studies linked back to only their single primary service. Each now links to three services supported by the actual lab work. Role wording explicitly identifies implementation, validation and documentation of synthetic work.
- **Inferred:** A visitor with a real problem benefits from knowing what information enables a scope. Each service now has a short, specific intake description and a second buyer-facing FAQ. These are explanations of possible scopes, not newly promised package terms.
- **Observed:** The About page mentioned a paid enterprise documentation review without linking to its commercial destination. That link is added; private client details remain private. A general services/pricing path is also added.
- **Observed:** Homepage secondary services were named as plain text except Proxmox. They are now direct links without changing layout or the hero.
- **Observed:** Pricing explains scope-led quotation and deliberately publishes no invented rate. It now explains quote inputs and an assessment option when implementation uncertainty is too high.

## Conversion path and limits

- **Observed:** Contact provides personal review language without a fixed response-time promise, an email alternative, geographic/remote clarity, and a service-aware inquiry form. No booking provider exists in the reviewed flow. The service CTA's existing preselection parameters are preserved.
- **Implemented:** The new scoping guidance asks for a sanitized summary and keeps credentials/private data out of the inquiry. It adds no mandatory form fields or additional gating step.
- **Inferred:** These changes can improve relevance, evidence discovery and inquiry completeness. They do not establish an increase in organic traffic, inquiry rate or client revenue. Aggregate analytics cannot prove an individual visitor's full journey.
- **Recommended:** Classify real inquiries by relevance, service, landing page/search source where actually recorded, and eventual business outcome. Review queries and behavior after enough clean observations instead of rewriting titles each week.
